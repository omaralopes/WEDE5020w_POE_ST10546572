Bulk order and catering enquiry

Feeding a team, a party or a whole street? Tell us what you need and we will instantly estimate the cost and check whether your date is available.

How it works

  1. Pick an item and quantity. Orders of 10 or more get 10% off; 25 or more get 15% off.
  2. Choose collection or delivery. Delivery is R35 within 15 km of Morningside and free over R500.
  3. Choose a date and time. We trade Tuesday to Sunday. Bulk orders need 2 days' notice and catering 5 days.
  4. Get your estimate. We confirm the final quote by email within one working day.

Bringing the grill to you

For events we set up the full smash station on site. A R250 set-up fee covers the grill, gas and crew, and we need a flat 3 m × 3 m space with access for the trailer.

Not a bulk order? Use the contact form for general questions or order from the menu.
