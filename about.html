About us

The story of how The Burger Bar came to be, and the people who keep the grill going in Durban.

Who we are

Business history

The Burger Bar started as a Durban mobile eatery with a simple idea: take American-inspired smash burgers to the people. Since 2016 we have been trading at local spots such as 246 Currie Road in Morningside.

We built the brand on the grill, not on a sit-down dining room. That mobile identity still shapes everything we do, from weekday cravings to busy trading days across Durban.

Mission statement

To serve fresh, high-quality, American-inspired smash burgers that are halal, flavourful and made to order, while bringing a fun, high-energy food experience to Durban and surrounding communities.

Vision statement

To become KwaZulu-Natal's most loved smash-burger brand by remaining excellent on the grill, easy to find online and available for everyday cravings.

Halal smash-burger preparation

Every burger is prepared with halal-certified ingredients. Our beef and chicken are sourced from trusted suppliers, and we keep preparation on the grill clear and consistent so families, students and halaal diners can order with confidence.

Burgers are smashed and cooked to order. We toast the buns, layer fresh toppings and finish each burger with house sauces. If you have an allergen concern, please ask the team when you order.
